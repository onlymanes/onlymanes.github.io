隐私政策

Privacy Policy

运营主体：onlymanes。本政策说明紫微星故事页（ziwei.html）会收集哪些信息、用来做什么、保留多久，以及如何联系我们。

Operator: onlymanes. This policy explains what information the Ziwei Star story page (ziwei.html) collects, what it is used for, how long it is kept, and how to contact us.

一、我们收集什么

1. What We Collect

  • 出生年月日
  • 出生时间（选填）
  • 出生地（选填）
  • 性别（选填）
  • 孩子昵称（可不填）
  • 一句话想法（选填，会原样放进故事里）
  • 想象题答案（选填，例如“如果前世在人间，会在哪里出生”）
  • Date of birth
  • Time of birth (optional)
  • Place of birth (optional)
  • Gender (optional)
  • Child's nickname (optional — you may leave it blank)
  • A one-sentence thought (optional; shown in the story as you wrote it)
  • Answers to imagination questions (optional, e.g. “If you had a past life in this world, where would you have been born?”)

我们不收集姓名、身份证号、电话号码、地址、账号密码，也不收集精确位置。

We do not collect your real name, ID number, phone number, address, account passwords, or precise location.

每次打开页面时，还会记录一次访问：IP 地址的 SHA-256 哈希（不存明文）、浏览器信息、访问路径、来源页面、语言、时区、屏幕尺寸、是否触屏，用于访客计数和每日 1 次的测算限制。

Each time you open the page, we also record one visit: a SHA-256 hash of your IP address (not the plaintext IP), browser information, the page path, referrer, language, timezone, screen size, and whether the device is touch-capable — used for visitor counting and the once-per-day test limit.

二、用来做什么

2. How We Use It

以上信息仅用于生成这一次的故事。我们不做用户画像、不跨站追踪、不投广告。但会按 IP 哈希计数访客、执行每日 1 次的测算限制。

This information is used only to generate this one story. We do not build user profiles, do not track you across sites, and do not serve ads. We do count visitors by IP hash and enforce the once-per-day test limit.

三、保留多久

3. How Long We Keep It

出生信息会话结束即删除、不落库；故事生成后，相关输入不会留在服务器上。每日限测的记录最多保留 48 小时；访客统计以日聚合形式保存，代码中没有设置过期时间。

Birth information is deleted when the session ends and is not stored; once the story is generated, your input does not remain on our servers. The daily-limit record is kept for at most 48 hours; visitor statistics are kept as daily aggregates with no expiry set in code.

四、数据在哪里处理

4. Where Data Is Processed

你的输入经 Cloudflare Worker 处理（节点位于美国 / 加拿大），发送给 Google Gemini 生成这一次的故事；若 Gemini 当时不可用或被限流，会改发给 DeepSeek（备用模型）生成，同样只为这一次的故事。我们不向任何第三方出售个人信息。

Your input is processed through a Cloudflare Worker (nodes in the United States / Canada) and sent to Google Gemini to generate this one story. If Gemini is unavailable or rate-limited at that moment, it is instead sent to DeepSeek (the fallback model) to generate the story, again only for this one story. We do not sell personal information to any third party.

五、未成年人

5. Minors

为未成年子女使用本服务，须由监护人同意。孩子昵称可不填；如填写，请只写昵称，不要写真实姓名。

Use of this service for a minor child requires the consent of a parent or guardian. The child's nickname is optional; if you fill it in, please use a nickname only, not the child's real name.

六、删除与联系

6. Deletion and Contact

如需删除相关信息，或对本政策有任何疑问，请发邮件到 onlymanes@gmail.com，我们会尽快回复。

If you would like information deleted, or have any questions about this policy, please email onlymanes@gmail.com and we will reply as soon as possible.
